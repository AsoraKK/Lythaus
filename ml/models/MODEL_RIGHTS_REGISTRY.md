# Model rights diligence

This register separates repository code, checkpoints, foundation encoders,
training data, dependencies, commercial use, distillation, and redistribution.
An Apache-2.0 repository entry is not evidence that its weights or training
data are commercially cleared.

Current default: SAFE, GRIP, DIRE, LaRE², and ADRD remain undownloaded and
non-training until all required rights fields are verified. Their upstream
research claims are not Lythaus benchmark results. The deterministic spectral
and camera branches are Lythaus-owned evidence implementations with no model
artefact dependency.

Large artefacts must live in the external WP003 cache with source URL, release
or commit, byte size, SHA-256, licence evidence reference, and approval status.
CI must never download them automatically.

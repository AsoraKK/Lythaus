# ADR 003: Lythaus Current State

**Status:** Accepted repository architecture; production launch remains NO-GO pending the gates below.

**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Decision

Lythaus uses Cloudflare Workers as its only backend runtime and PlanetScale
PostgreSQL through the existing Hyperdrive bindings as its canonical database.
The active backend consists of the public API Worker, protected admin API
Worker, and Jobs Worker. Existing R2, Queue, Workflow, KV, Access, and email
resources may be reused only through the resource registry and approval gates.
No Azure path, Hive integration, or provider fallback is part of the active
architecture.

Initial identity is guest access plus email registration, verification,
password login, refresh rotation, logout, and password reset. Google, Apple,
World ID, Entra, B2C, generic OAuth selection, and invite-only identity are not
active product modes. Production email registration must fail closed behind
Turnstile and the required authentication secrets; deployment evidence remains
a launch gate.

## Product-integrity invariants

- Public authorship labels are categorical. Human-authored public content may
  qualify for authorship reputation. AI-assisted text is NFC-normalised and
  trimmed, is limited to 249 user-perceived Unicode characters, and earns no
  authorship reputation. AI-generated public content is blocked and may appear
  only in private author feedback supporting an appeal.
- Subscription entitlements are Free, Premium, and Black. They provide one,
  two, and three custom feeds respectively; only Black receives News Board.
  Editorial is earned, merit-based, revocable, and never a subscription tier.
- Reputation is the versioned private Level 0-5 policy with accountability,
  contribution, conduct, sourcing, authenticity, and review-reliability
  pillars. Promotion requires time, distributed activity, qualifying human
  contribution, pillar gates, good standing, and anti-gaming eligibility.
  Popularity, raw volume, reactions, follows, and payment cannot buy trust.
- Appeals use five independently assigned trained reviewers. A known related
  account group may occupy at most one seat. Votes are immutable after submit;
  recusals replace the reviewer; all five valid reviews and a weighted majority
  of at least 60% are required. At most one trained Level 5 reviewer may have
  weight two. A trained Editorial or journalist adjudicator must confirm the
  result, with two adjudicators for high-risk cases. There is no public vote,
  timed auto-resolution, staff override, or moderator override.
- Posts, comments, replies, reactions, follows, blocks, mutes, bookmarks,
  custom feeds, flags, notifications, and privacy requests enforce ownership,
  block/mute visibility, cursor, idempotency, rate, and tier boundaries in the
  authoritative Worker rather than trusting the client.
- The Activity and Audit Ledger is private, append-oriented, idempotent, and
  metadata-allowlisted. It records authoritative semantic outcomes, not raw
  content, credentials, secrets, or clickstream telemetry.

## Authenticity boundary

Lythaus Authenticity AI is provider-neutral, Lythaus-owned, and located under
`packages/authenticity/`. `@cf/openai/gpt-oss-20b` through Workers AI is an
evidence-orchestration candidate only. Model output cannot independently allow,
block, remove, rank, publish, change reputation, or resolve an appeal. Dataset
rights and provenance checks fail closed, materialisation is bounded to
authorised external paths, and no user-content training or provider mutation is
authorised by this ADR.

## Privacy and security

UUIDv7 identifiers are generated by the application and stored as PostgreSQL
`uuid`. Private accountability names and email data use the approved encrypted
and keyed-lookup paths. Authenticated and personalised responses are private and
`no-store`; public caching must be explicit and isolated from viewer state.
Errors exposed to users are allowlisted and opaque. DSR export, download,
deletion, subject-location reconciliation, retention, legal holds, and retained
governance pseudonymisation cover the product-integrity records without copying
private names into logs, activity metadata, or outbox payloads.

## Evidence boundary and launch gates

This ADR governs repository architecture; it does not claim deployed readiness.
Production launch remains NO-GO until the full migration chain is validated on
PostgreSQL 17 and explicitly approved for `main`; exact-SHA CI is green; Worker
deployments, bindings, Access, Turnstile, and required secrets are verified;
private DSR export/delete/legal-hold drills pass; reviewer and adjudicator
training and conflict operations are approved; strict feed p95 and cache
isolation are measured on release-like infrastructure; incident and rollback
evidence is retained; and any disabled queue enablement receives explicit
no-cost approval.

The consolidation release chain is `main with verified release governance ->
reviewed SHA -> canonical Cloudflare release -> verified PlanetScale state`.
GitHub native branch protection is recorded as `UNAVAILABLE_BY_PLAN` for the
private repository, so `.github/workflows/production-release.yml` enforces the
compensating controls: merged-PR provenance, resolved conversations, linear
history, prior-release ancestry, exact-SHA CI/security, and provider evidence.
The workflow coordinates exact-SHA surface deployments, while `docs/evidence/`
records sanitized provider and branch evidence. A missing provider capability is
`UNKNOWN/BLOCKED`; green repository CI alone never grants production approval.

## Supersession

Active implementation, policy, runbook, OpenAPI, generated-client, and UI
documentation must conform to this ADR. Historical records may describe retired
architectures only when clearly stored or labelled as history.

## Configuration and budgets

Master `AUTHENTICITY_BETA_ENABLED` defaults false in public API, admin API, Jobs and runtime. Private KV `authenticity-beta-v1` also requires `enabled:true`, an unexpired `expiresAt` no more than seven days ahead, a UUID allowlist, component switches, approval receipt hashes (`rightsApproval`, `budgetApproval`, `runtimeApproval`), image digest and preprocessing hash. Expiry stops new work while retaining owner read/delete access. `sourceHistoryHashes` must stay empty for ordinary unknown-history uploads. Only documented, approved lossless fixtures may be listed.

Safety remains independently enforced. `AUTHENTICITY_BETA_STORAGE_ENABLED` keeps expiry/deletion cleanup active even when inference is disabled. Disabling the beta does not disable authentication/Safety or publish media. Reads, cancellation and deletion remain available under their ownership rules.

Three ambiguous SAFE attempts across cases in 15 minutes pause further work. Three adviser failures in that window disable optional advice while preserving the deterministic result. Budget/global/provider kill switches apply before every step. Each consumed external attempt stays consumed after a crash; a retry cannot promise exactly-once provider billing.

The existing US$10/month total incremental experiment ceiling is binding. Beta is additionally limited to $2 with 20% metering headroom; the global admission reserve stops at $8. There is no per-case default: absent a measured, approved `caseEnvelope`, admission returns `beta_measured_reservation_required`. The old $0.50/case default is removed. Daily ceilings of five/user and twenty/global are upper bounds, not promised capacity. Unknown billed cost remains null; committed estimates continue consuming budget. Broader application variables are not a substitute for the stricter beta checks.

The proposed smoke envelope is at most 16 SAFE attempts, two advisory calls on distinct cases, 30 active Container minutes and $0.50 reserved. This is not yet a certified price or permission to spend. Include Safety, startup/idle provisioned memory/disk, CPU, R2, queues, Workers/DO, logs and possible retries. Do not assume unused included allowances. Application admission limits are not a provider invoice cap.

The loopback CPU runner consumes all sixteen planned SAFE attempts if it completes. Later deployed-app acceptance therefore needs separately bounded admission in the approved activation envelope; it cannot silently reuse or extend that smoke allowance. Keep cumulative real attempt counts across both tracks and all failed/ambiguous starts.

`scripts/authenticity/beta-release-policy.mjs` separates the total smoke envelope from recurring case admission. Both include the full configured 1,800-second runtime lifetime, provisioned memory/disk, bounded CPU, advice and ancillary services. `caseEnvelope` binds a measurement receipt, observed whole-case duration and attributable per-service upper bounds. Safety, private image registry and database bounds must be supplied explicitly. Reservation rounds the estimate plus headroom upward to cents. Capacity is `floor(min(1.60 - betaCommitted, 8 - sharedCommitted) / caseReservation)`, clamped at zero; the test fixture's $0.06 and 26 cases are not operational measurements or a beta capacity claim.

For R2 and Durable Objects, the calculator computes the delta between rounded account-period totals after verified included units. It does not round every case as if it were a new monthly invoice. Workers, Queues, KV and logs use proportional rates; Containers use 10 ms billing increments. Unknown allowances/aggregation state retain conservative full-unit charges. With unknown usage, R2 Class A alone reserves $4.50 and a new DO duration unit $12.50, so the approved envelope cannot be certified. Usage evidence must be at most one hour old, and activated KV admission expires at the earlier of the approval or that evidence's one-hour validity. Refresh under protected controls; an old allowance cannot fund seven days of admission. These are estimates, never observed bills.

Admission commits a reservation before external work. Failed pre-admission setup releases its unused reservation; cancellation/deletion never assumes provider charges are zero. `reconcileBetaCost` is an internal administrator-only, audited transaction: after deletion, storage purge and no active work, a final attributable billing receipt must account for every completed/ambiguous attempt. Unsettled provider or storage charges keep the reserve committed. PostgreSQL protocol fixtures exercise this without writing PlanetScale production.

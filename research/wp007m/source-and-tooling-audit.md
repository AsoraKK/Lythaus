# WP007M source and tooling audit

Audited 2026-09-19; updated after independent code and discovery-only inspection. Three research works and the official NumPy/SciPy projects were reviewed in the first pass; no additional literature search in this update. No heldout media or heldout outcomes were opened. Numerical checks are recorded in [independent-audit.json](independent-audit.json).

## Primary evidence and novelty boundary

| Primary source | Verified relevance and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Foi, Trimeche, Katkovnik and Egiazarian, **Practical Poissonian-Gaussian noise modeling and fitting for single-image raw-data**, IEEE TIP (2008), [DOI and university record](https://researchportal.tuni.fi/fi/publications/practical-poissonian-gaussian-noise-modeling-and-fitting-for-sing/). | Signal-dependent sensor noise and clipping are established prior art. The authors' [presentation, slide 8](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/papers/Foi-EUSIPCO08-Slides.pdf) gives the pre-clipping variance law `sigma²(y) = a*y + b`. Its input is sensor raw data; it does not establish that residual energy in a processed JPEG follows that law or identifies synthetic origin. |
| Farid and Lyu, **Higher-order Wavelet Statistics and their Application to Digital Forensics**, IEEE Workshop on Statistical Analysis in Computer Vision (2003), [author-hosted paper](https://farid.berkeley.edu/downloads/publications/sacv03.pdf), Sections 2, 3.2 and 4. | The method measures wavelet-subband moments and moments of log prediction errors using spatial, orientation and scale neighbors. It applies a trained classifier to photographs versus computer graphics. Cross-scale magnitude dependence and forensic use are therefore established. The authors explicitly discuss counterattacks that match these statistics. This is not validation on present-day generators. |
| Portilla and Simoncelli, **A parametric texture model based on joint statistics of complex wavelet coefficients**, IJCV (2000), [author abstract and reprint links](https://www.cns.nyu.edu/~lcv/pubs/makeAbs.php?loc=Portilla99), DOI `10.1023/A:1026553619983`. | Joint coefficient statistics across nearby positions, orientations and scales constrain texture synthesis. Thus cross-scale dependence also describes image content and can be synthesized. This is a texture model, not a sensor-provenance detector. |

**Actual mechanism:** the revised plan rejects noise/intensity coupling as duplicate EF2 work. `wp007m_probe.py` computes three levels of orthonormal Haar coefficients from luminance of a centered native RGB crop. For each adjacent level pair it averages rank correlations of absolute coefficients over measurable orientations, replicating each parent onto its child grid. The frozen primary score is `abs(dependency12-dependency23)`, with higher values the challenge direction. This is spatial cross-scale magnitude dependence, without intensity conditioning or a fitted noise model. It extends established wavelet statistics; neither these sources nor this bounded review establish unpublished novelty. It differs from [WP007K's marginal summaries](../wp007k/feature-definition.json), but distinct construction alone does not demonstrate incremental forensic utility.

## Five important independent findings

1. **Lineage gates are incomplete, although current discovery hashes pass.** In the inspected `wp007m_probe.py`, `select()` accepts discovery rows without validating a complete unique source/condition matrix, their role, code/cohort/input hashes, or eligible negative rights. `joined()` permits row fields to override cohort metadata. `extract()` checks only existence of the rule file before heldout extraction. In `wp007m_analyze.py`, the decision self-hash is checked, but the discovery input is not compared with `discoveryRowsHash`, nor are row lineage and exact expected membership validated; count and uniqueness alone are insufficient. Independently checking the existing discovery rows passed all these applicable hash/membership checks. These are missing enforcement gates, not proof of corrupted current observations. The frozen cohort's selection-code hash also differs from the extraction hash; preserve stage-specific provenance rather than claiming one unchanged file covered both stages.
2. **The shifted parent is an alignment control, not a calibrated randomization null.** `dependence()` uses one deterministic toroidal shift per parent band: `(height//3,width//5)`. It preserves coefficient marginals but need not destroy periodic or large-scale content correspondence. There is no permutation distribution, significance estimate, or independent extra sample. Replicated parent values and neighboring coefficients also do not create independent observations. The primary absolute difference can shrink even when both individual dependencies change; inspect both level-pair controls descriptively. Do not change the frozen null or select a better shift from outcomes. Missing orientations are omitted from each mean and may differ across level pairs; orientation coverage is not recorded.
3. **Discovery does not support the frozen high-score direction.** Independent pairwise and average-rank AUROC calculations agree: native primary AUROC is **0.3215993**, with 39 synthetic and 59 measurable negative parents; one negative has an unavailable candidate value. The native baseline AUROC is **0.6542735** on 39/60 parents. Across declared conditions the candidate AUROCs remain below 0.5. This is a bounded discovery finding, not a heldout conclusion. Reversing direction, replacing the scalar, or selecting a condition from these synthetic outcomes would violate the amendment. Different missing-value denominators must remain visible in comparisons.
4. **Zero discovery false positives is built into reference selection.** The verified candidate threshold is `0.3487158244046029`; baseline is `2.2144267960080066`. Both equal their discovery-negative maximum across conditions, and strict equality is not positive. Source-worst-case decisions independently reproduce 0/60 negative parents, comprising 0/40 photographs and 0/20 hard negatives. Nominal two-sided Wilson upper endpoints are 6.0172%, 8.7622% and 16.1125%, respectively. Those are verified arithmetic, not valid external performance evidence for a threshold chosen on these same negatives. No 1% FPR claim follows. Missing scores count as no evidence in decisions but are excluded from AUROC.
5. **Use actual cohort denominators and provenance.** Frozen metadata contains discovery 40 PHOTO / 20 HARD / 39 SYNTHETIC and heldout 60 / 30 / 59, rather than the plan's intended 40 / 20 / 40 and 60 / 40 / 80. The code caps each hard subtype at five; the six heldout subtypes therefore produce at most 30 before exclusions. PHOTO is Unsplash-derived despite the `CAMERA_NATIVE` subtype; camera-model grouping does not establish physical-device identity or pristine capture. HARD consists of owned procedural templates, including the CGI-labeled entries, not a representative conventional-CGI corpus. Source-level denominators avoid counting transformations as new images but do not eliminate within-template or camera-model clustering.

**Checks actually run:** 792 discovery rows form the exact 99-parent by eight-condition matrix; discovery bytes match both run and decision-freeze hashes, cohort/rule self-hashes verify, and per-row cohort/code/input hashes match metadata. Isolated source Haar calculations match an independent matrix transform and energy check exactly on a deterministic numerical fixture. Wilson endpoints match an independently written formula within `2.23e-16`; rank and pairwise AUROC agree, including a half-credit-tie fixture yielding `0.875`. Source-worst-case candidate/baseline decisions were independently recomputed. No extraction, model inference or full SciPy/sklearn integration was rerun.

## Confounders and best falsification

**Camera/ISP hypotheses to challenge:** gain, clipping, demosaicing, denoising, sharpening and tone mapping can change residual amplitude or spatial dependence. Foi's raw-domain model does not justify treating a processed-image slope as a physical camera parameter. **Content/CGI hypotheses:** edges, repetitive texture and depth-of-field can dominate a residual; conventional CGI and texture synthesis can exhibit cross-scale structure. These are inference-driven controls motivated by the raw-domain limitation and texture/forensic prior art above, not measured WP007M findings.

**Best falsification:** use already-authorized images in a paired source-by-processing challenge. For every camera, conventional-CGI and generated source, apply the same predeclared JPEG/resize conditions, including encoder, subsampling, resize kernel, output geometry and operation order. Compare each source's processing-induced score change with the between-origin difference under matched conditions; retain source-level grouping rather than counting descendants as independent images. Include untouched inputs because recompression cannot erase unknown earlier processing history.

For this frozen run, compare the declared paired transformations, individual level-pair dependencies and fixed-shift controls without adding or optimizing perturbations. Treat disappearance under matched processing, performance reproduced by the marginal baseline, or persistence in the shifted control as evidence against the intended explanation. An additional denoise/sharpen challenge or representative conventional-CGI panel would require a separately declared future study. Persistence alone would support further measurement research, not sensor provenance.

## Permissive tooling and data boundary

- **SciPy/NumPy:** official [SciPy license](https://github.com/scipy/scipy/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) and [NumPy license](https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) provide permissive three-clause BSD terms. Actual code uses SciPy `rankdata` and NumPy arithmetic/replication/roll, not the Gaussian-filter or random-permutation approach proposed in the first audit. Preserve notices and exact dependency versions. No package was installed for this audit.
- **Do not adopt Foi's reference implementation on an assumed permissive license:** the [author software page](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/sensornoise.html) explicitly restricts unauthorized industrial/profit-oriented use. Use the papers as references and implement the bounded statistic independently with the approved numerical tools.
- **Amended rights boundary:** primary feature, high-score direction, aggregation and strict comparison are preselected. Only the approved photographic/owned negative references set the maximum cutoff; synthetic labels cannot choose features, direction, weights or thresholds. No trained weights are produced. The inspected selector implements that arithmetic, but should enforce the eligible-negative metadata gate explicitly. Descriptive matching or candidate distributions do not authorize selecting a new rule.

Source coverage remains unchanged: Farid/Lyu method and limitations were read directly; Portilla/Simoncelli's author abstract supports the stated scope; Foi metadata, author material and indexed excerpts were verified, but full-paper retrieval timed out. The follow-up read code, plan, cohort metadata, discovery-run metadata, decision freeze and discovery rows only. No heldout outcomes/media, models or source images were opened; no downloads or production changes occurred.

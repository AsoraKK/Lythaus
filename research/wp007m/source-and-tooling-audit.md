# WP007M source and tooling audit

Audited 2026-09-19. Bounded source review: three research works and the official NumPy/SciPy projects. This sidecar provides measurement guidance, not implementation, experimental results, or an unpublished-novelty claim.

## Primary evidence and novelty boundary

| Primary source | Verified relevance and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Foi, Trimeche, Katkovnik and Egiazarian, **Practical Poissonian-Gaussian noise modeling and fitting for single-image raw-data**, IEEE TIP (2008), [DOI and university record](https://researchportal.tuni.fi/fi/publications/practical-poissonian-gaussian-noise-modeling-and-fitting-for-sing/). | Signal-dependent sensor noise and clipping are established prior art. The authors' [presentation, slide 8](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/papers/Foi-EUSIPCO08-Slides.pdf) gives the pre-clipping variance law `sigma²(y) = a*y + b`. Its input is sensor raw data; it does not establish that residual energy in a processed JPEG follows that law or identifies synthetic origin. |
| Farid and Lyu, **Higher-order Wavelet Statistics and their Application to Digital Forensics**, IEEE Workshop on Statistical Analysis in Computer Vision (2003), [author-hosted paper](https://farid.berkeley.edu/downloads/publications/sacv03.pdf), Sections 2, 3.2 and 4. | The method measures wavelet-subband moments and moments of log prediction errors using spatial, orientation and scale neighbors. It applies a trained classifier to photographs versus computer graphics. Cross-scale magnitude dependence and forensic use are therefore established. The authors explicitly discuss counterattacks that match these statistics. This is not validation on present-day generators. |
| Portilla and Simoncelli, **A parametric texture model based on joint statistics of complex wavelet coefficients**, IJCV (2000), [author abstract and reprint links](https://www.cns.nyu.edu/~lcv/pubs/makeAbs.php?loc=Portilla99), DOI `10.1023/A:1026553619983`. | Joint coefficient statistics across nearby positions, orientations and scales constrain texture synthesis. Thus cross-scale dependence also describes image content and can be synthesized. This is a texture model, not a sensor-provenance detector. |

**Assessment:** WP007M can investigate a specified combination of intensity-conditioned residual/structure coupling, spatial nulls and processing controls. These sources do not establish that this combination is new, and this bounded review cannot establish absence of closer prior work. Compared with the repository's [WP007K feature definition](../wp007k/feature-definition.json), the useful distinction is a spatially paired, conditional measurement; adding more whole-image band-energy summaries would not make that distinction.

## Instrument and null audit

The following are audit recommendations and mathematical inferences, not claims that the cited papers validate WP007M:

- Predeclare a fine-scale residual-energy map, a coarser structure map and local intensity strata. Measure their within-stratum association and aggregate with fixed rules. Keep marginal energies and stratum coverage as diagnostics. A difference-of-Gaussians implementation should be called a multiscale residual proxy; it does not reproduce either paper's wavelet transform.
- State the null precisely: residual locations are exchangeable relative to structure locations within the declared intensity strata. Permute already-computed residual-map blocks against fixed structure/intensity maps. Shuffling pixels before filtering creates a different image and tests a different hypothesis. Use blocks large enough to preserve relevant local dependence; record block size, admissible assignments, masks, seed and permutation count. If multiple residual scales form one observation, move them together; independently shuffling a scale instead tests interscale dependence.
- Report the observed association, null distribution and observed-minus-null effect. Spatial autocorrelation and nonstationary texture make unrestricted pixel exchangeability doubtful. A block-null percentile is descriptive unless its exchangeability assumptions are justified; a small permutation tail probability is not a probability of synthetic origin. Compare unrestricted and block nulls to detect sensitivity to spatial dependence.
- Shared filters and overlapping neighborhoods can produce coupling even for independent input noise. Include a fixed procedural flat/ramp/edge control with independent noise and run the identical extraction. Exclude filter-border support and clipping by frozen rules; mark constant maps or insufficient strata unmeasurable rather than assigning evidence scores.

## Confounders and best falsification

**Camera/ISP hypotheses to challenge:** gain, clipping, demosaicing, denoising, sharpening and tone mapping can change residual amplitude or spatial dependence. Foi's raw-domain model does not justify treating a processed-image slope as a physical camera parameter. **Content/CGI hypotheses:** edges, repetitive texture and depth-of-field can dominate a residual; conventional CGI and texture synthesis can exhibit cross-scale structure. These are inference-driven controls motivated by the raw-domain limitation and texture/forensic prior art above, not measured WP007M findings.

**Best falsification:** use already-authorized images in a paired source-by-processing challenge. For every camera, conventional-CGI and generated source, apply the same predeclared JPEG/resize conditions, including encoder, subsampling, resize kernel, output geometry and operation order. Compare each source's processing-induced score change with the between-origin difference under matched conditions; retain source-level grouping rather than counting descendants as independent images. Include untouched inputs because recompression cannot erase unknown earlier processing history.

Then test a fixed, label-independent denoise/sharpen or intensity-dependent noise perturbation, where existing permissions allow it. Reject a sensor-origin interpretation if ordinary camera processing moves the score through the apparent origin separation, if conventional CGI yields the same claimed signal, or if the difference vanishes under intensity/structure matching or block nulls. Persistence would support further measurement research only. No numerical success threshold or effect size is established by this audit.

## Permissive tooling and data boundary

- **SciPy:** official [repository license](https://github.com/scipy/scipy/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) permits source/binary redistribution with conditions. [`ndimage.gaussian_filter`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter.html) provides Gaussian smoothing and derivatives with explicit boundary handling. Use floating-point input/output: the documentation warns that intermediate precision follows output dtype. Freeze scales, support and boundary mode.
- **NumPy:** official [repository license](https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) likewise has permissive three-clause BSD terms. [`Generator.permutation`](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/random/generated/numpy.random.Generator.permutation.html) can permute block-index vectors; its default axis on a matrix permutes rows, not arbitrary spatial blocks. Record the installed versions, generator and seed. Preserve required notices and check bundled dependency notices when packaging; no package was installed here.
- **Do not adopt Foi's reference implementation on an assumed permissive license:** the [author software page](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/sensornoise.html) explicitly restricts unauthorized industrial/profit-oriented use. Use the papers as references and implement the bounded statistic independently with the approved numerical tools.
- **Evaluation-only means measurement only:** freeze filters, strata, null, aggregation, direction and decision rules before evaluation. Do not fit classifiers/predictors, learn normalization, tune thresholds, select features or optimize perturbations using evaluation-only images or labels. Per-image summaries under fixed rules are measurements, not permission to train. Keep procedural controls separate from evaluation media.

Coverage: Farid/Lyu method and limitations were read directly; Portilla/Simoncelli's author abstract supports the stated scope. Foi's metadata, author material and indexed paper excerpts were verified; full-paper retrieval timed out. No models, code archives or media were downloaded, and no experiments were run.

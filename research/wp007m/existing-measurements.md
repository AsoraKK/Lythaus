| Existing forensics.ts wavelets | One-level Haar-like values/marginal feature vectors, no explicit spatial parent-child copula. | Three-level magnitude-rank co-occurrence and its loss across scales; fixed spatial shift preserves every band's marginal values. |
| L PRNU residual pilot | Weak content-contaminated residual correlations, not an enrolled-device qualification. | No claim that wavelet dependence is sensor identity. |

The new quantity is a joint spatial relationship, not an additional frequency-energy bin. It is still an EF4-like statistical measurement until AI-specificity and independence are established. The same scene edges and procedural textures can generate it; the hypothesis must survive hard negatives and matched processing. No error independence is assumed.

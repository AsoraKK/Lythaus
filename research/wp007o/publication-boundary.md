The owner authorizes only reusable methodology/tooling and fabricated fixture validation. Public comparative disclosure remains blocked. No new permission to disclose scientific evidence is inferred.

The public branch is rooted at the existing research parent, not at the authoritative private O commit. Publication review must include every new commit and its diff, not merely the final file tree: deleted content remains visible in Git history. Only the new methodology branch is pushed; private O refs and tags are not pushed.

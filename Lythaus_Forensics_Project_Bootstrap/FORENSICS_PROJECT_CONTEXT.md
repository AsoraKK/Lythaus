Lythaus is building a human-first authenticity system, not a single binary AI detector. **Safety**, **Forensics** and **Judge** are separate. Forensics collects five mandatory evidence families: EF1 file/provenance, EF2 physical camera acquisition, EF3 generative forensics, EF4 spectral/transformation stability, EF5 reconstruction/local manipulation. Camera-origin and synthetic-origin evidence are independent; low camera evidence never means AI by itself. The first Judge candidate is `gpt-oss-20b` on Cloudflare Workers AI, but no model can directly enforce policy. Deterministic application code owns final action. Public labels are Human-authored, AI-assisted, AI-generated and Under review; numeric confidence remains internal.

The locked runtime architecture is Cloudflare-first: Workers, R2, Queues, Workflows, KV, Containers, Workers AI/AI Gateway, with PlanetScale PostgreSQL authoritative through Hyperdrive and GitHub canonical for source/CI. Azure and Hive are retired and prohibited from active restoration. Incremental experimental infrastructure is capped at **US$10/month** without new owner approval. Production authenticity enforcement is still disabled.

Current data assets: Unsplash Dataset Lite (~25,000 images) has been acquired and owner-approved for Lythaus use under the exact Dataset Lite terms; those terms clearly permit internal business ML training but external API/derived-weight/commercial-deployment scope must remain separately documented before broadening rights gates. The owner began downloading the CSAFE Multi-camera Smartphone Image Database (~123.62 GB, ~50,000 JPEGs, 60 smartphones, CC BY 4.0); completion/local path must be confirmed and the dataset formally registered. The owner also has 1,000+ potential Lythaus-original photos that should become a Class C known-camera corpus after provenance/rights/privacy ingestion.

Current model policy: Lythaus spectral/phase and deterministic camera branches are the approved first baselines. SAFE remains a conditional teacher; GRIP CLIP an independent control/possible teacher; DIRE/LaRE²/ADRD are reconstruction research candidates; none should be downloaded or used for training until code/weight/foundation/data/derived-rights gates are recorded. Student v0 is a future compact CPU fusion model over approved teacher outputs plus deterministic evidence; do not train it yet.

Benchmark v0 target: 320 source images — 80 camera, 80 AI-generated, 40 CGI/digital art, 20 scans/scientific/medical, 30 screenshots/composites, 40 partial edits, 30 unseen holdout — plus controlled transformations. Required metrics include FPR/FNR, precision/recall/F1, AUROC/AUPRC, calibration, abstention, per-generator/unseen-generator, hard-negative, transformation stability, latency/memory/CPU/cost. Human-content FPR target is <=1%; material subgroup rates >2% require mitigation/review.

Local compute is the HP 15-fc0xxx with Ryzen 7 7730U/16 GB RAM. Treat as CPU-only. ECO-TRAIN: 40% target CPU, 50% ceiling, four workers initially, six only after qualification, 6 GB process RAM, >=4 GB free RAM, >=80 GB free disk, AC required. Pause 75°C, stop 85°C, resume below 65°C for 120s. Valid CPU-package telemetry has not yet been obtained; a 0°C Tctl/Tdie reading was rejected. **Unattended training remains prohibited.**
